View Example: Engineering Student Summer Plan ▼

Rising sophomore (after 9th grade)

Goal Build foundation in engineering + start competition track
Program Options Reach: CMU SAMS (free, for underrepresented students)
Target: SSP (~$7,500 with financial aid available)
Safety: Local university engineering camps
Competitions Science Fair (local to state), FIRST Robotics, Congressional App Challenge, AMC 10
Projects Arduino/Raspberry Pi project, personal coding on GitHub, online courses (MIT OpenCourseWare)
Timeline Sept-Dec: Join robotics, start projects, take online course
Jan-Mar: Apply to summer programs, science fair, take AMC 10
June-Aug: Attend program OR intensive project work

Rising junior (after 10th grade): critical summer

Goal Most important summer for admissions. Aim for selective program OR deep independent work.
Top Programs Reach: RSI (~3% acceptance, Dec 10 deadline), MITES (~2%, Feb 1 deadline)
Target: Clark Scholars (FREE + stipend), Simons (FREE + stipend), BU RISE ($6,500)
Safety: Email professors at state universities for research opportunities
If Accepted to Program Go beyond requirements, build mentor relationships for strong rec letters, continue research for ISEF/Regeneron STS senior year
If Not Accepted Option A: Email 20+ professors for independent research
Option B: Deep technical project (app, robot, circuit design)
Option C: Combination of research + personal project
Competitions USACO (advance Bronze to Silver to Gold), Science Olympiad, FIRST Robotics (leadership role), Science Fair to ISEF, AMC 12 to AIME
Key Deadlines Nov 6: Regeneron STS (if research ready)
Dec 10: RSI application
Feb 1: MITES application
Jan-May: Science fairs, ISEF qualification

Rising senior (after 11th grade)

Goal Balance summer experience with college application prep. Less pressure than junior summer.
Options Continue research from junior summer
Paid engineering internship
Regular job while working on applications
Primary Focus June-July: Draft Common App essays, finalize activities list
August: Finish essays, prep for interviews, confirm rec letters
Final Competitions Regeneron STS: Nov 6 deadline, results in Jan (update colleges)
ISEF: Jan-March local fair to qualify
USACO: Push to Platinum if at Gold level

Note: This is a sample plan for an engineering-focused student. Your personalized summer strategy will be tailored to your specific interests, background, and goals.
